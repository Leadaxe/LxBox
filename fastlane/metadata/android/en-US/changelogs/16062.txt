Two crashes are gone from the core: switching networks while the tunnel was starting, and a TCP connection to an unreachable node. Both came from user reports with crash dumps.

First steps towards Android TV: the app now shows up in the TV launcher, and the two places that were outright broken there are fixed — importing from a file, and remote control navigation. There is no proper TV interface yet; the layout is still designed for a phone, so TV support stays best-effort.

Core updated to v1.14.0-lx.20-rc.3.
