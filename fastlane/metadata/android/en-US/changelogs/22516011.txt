Release candidate. OpenConnect (FortiGate and others): a form in Add server and on the Source tab of the node; sign-in codes and browser SSO through the Sign-in required notification. DNS settings are now a tab of the Routing screen; the DNS Settings menu item is gone. The new Tunnel ingress preset, on by default, rejects incoming connections from WireGuard, AWG, OpenVPN and OpenConnect peers unless the node allows them. JSON editor: line wrap. Subscriptions: announce text, emoji tag prefix.
