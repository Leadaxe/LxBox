A whole sing-box config now imports at once — nodes, auto-select groups and detour chains — so moving over from sing-box no longer means setting everything up by hand.

Rule sets refresh on their own schedule, with a per-rule update interval (daily to yearly) and a visible age indicator.

Fixes for the "switched subscription" flow: a change could fail to reach the config, the tunnel could stick in Connected with a dead Stop button, and freshly added nodes could stay without latency measurements. A pinned preset no longer loses its place at the top of the rules list.

Full changelog: https://github.com/Leadaxe/LxBox/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
