Rule sets now refresh on their own schedule, with a per-rule update interval (daily to yearly) and a visible age indicator.

Fixes for the "switched subscription" flow: a change could fail to reach the config, the tunnel could stick in Connected with a dead Stop button, and freshly added nodes could stay without latency measurements.
